Research Projects

Single Photon Detectors

Probing the dark universe. We are characterizing Superconducting Nanowire Single Photon Detectors (SNSPDs) in extreme, high-magnetic-field environments to unlock new pathways for dark matter detection and fundamental physics discoveries.

Superconducting Qubits

Forging a new baseline for quantum computing. We spearheaded the design, simulation, fabrication, and testing of the first proof-of-concept superconducting qubit at Missouri S&T, laying the groundwork for future device development.

Superconducting Quantum Sensors

Unlocking the Cosmic Microwave Background. Operating at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), I engineered the mechanical cryogenic polarization response system for Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detectors (MKIDs) operating below 100mK.

Self-Colpitts Oscillator

Dynamically tracking the unmeasurable. We developed a novel cryogenic AC magnetic measurement technique utilizing self-oscillating LC circuits to map elusive magnetic phenomena and dynamical changes in topological superconductors.

Interferometer for Pulse Probe Experiment

Lasers are imperfect—until characterized. We built a high-precision Michelson-Morley style interferometer to rigorously decode and characterize laser properties, including frequency, wavelength, and pulse-width dynamics.

Electron Beam Evaporator

Engineering precision from the ground up. We assembled a complete ultra-high vacuum e-beam lithography system, engineering the source and beam focus for next-generation, high-purity metal thin-film deposition.

Bio-Inspired Floating Foundations

Harnessing deep-ocean wind through biomimicry. To tap into the vast wind potential of the deep ocean, we designed, modeled, and tested nature-inspired floating offshore wind turbine (FOTW) foundations engineered to withstand turbulent marine environments.

Digital Factory for 3-D Printing

Welcome to Industry 5.0. We integrated additive manufacturing, resin printing, and metal deposition into a unified digital factory where every machine is actively controlled and monitored in real-time via its digital twin.
